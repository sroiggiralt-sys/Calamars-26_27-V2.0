## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered specifically for coastal cephalopod anglers operating under moonlit rocky shores, jetties, and Mediterranean shallows across Mallorca. The aesthetic marries high-performance maritime instrumentation with the organic, hypnotic luminescence of deep-water cephalopods.

The personality balances field-ready ruggedness with technical sophistication. It evokes the quiet focus of late-night casting sessions: salt spray, calm dark swells, lunar cycles, and the phosphorescent strike of a squid jig cutting through the current. 

### Design Movements & Tone
- **Technical Night Instrumentation:** High-contrast interfaces optimized strictly for low-light legibility and preservation of natural night vision. Surfaces do not blast white light; instead, vital metrics emerge via selective phosphorescence.
- **Bioluminescent Accents:** Key points of interest, strikes, and active states glow with the electric radiance of squid bioluminescence and charged photoluminescent cloth jigs (*egis*).
- **Subtle Bathymetry & Tactical Rigor:** Structural lines resemble hydrographic charting, bathymetric contours, and depth soundings. Precision data visualization takes precedence over decorative fluff.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the chromatic conditions of nocturnal angling: bottomless Balearic sea depths punctuated by phosphorescent lures and ambient lunar reflections.

### Surface Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#070d18`):** Deep oceanic abyss providing absolute black-point contrast without causing retina shock.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#0a1124`):** Elevated base backdrop for sections and docked navigation panels.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#111c33`):** Primary card container fill, providing clear content partitioning.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (`#162442`):** Elevated widgets, interactive trays, sheet drawers, and active row items.
- **Subtle Structural Border (`#1e335a`):** Defined container margins, divider lines, and hydrographic grids.

### Luminescence & Functional Accents
- **Primary Phosphor (`#00e5bc` / `#14f1d9`):** Bioluminescent teal-cyan reserved for strike indicators, active GPS lock, high-confidence tide windows, and primary actions. Simulates charged UV/glow egis.
- **Lunar Amber (`#ff9e3b` / `#ff7733`):** Radiant warm tone representing moon phases, Solunar peak alerts, water temperature spikes, and jig color-tag markers.
- **Deep Red Alert (`#ff3b56`):** Emergency maritime warnings, barometric drops, and destructive wind shifts.

### Typography & Readability
- **Primary Text (`#f0f6fc`):** High-readability crisp seafoam white for crucial metrics, labels, and telemetry.
- **Secondary Text (`#8b9eb7`):** Desaturated nautical slate for contextual coordinates, historical averages, and secondary metadata.
- **Disabled / Grid Tint (`#374d6f`):** De-emphasized lines, contour tracings, and inactive states.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes rapid glanceability through salt-crusted phone screens, headlamp glare, or moonless twilight.

- **Primary Sans (`Inter`):** Deployed for all general UI prose, settings, and descriptive capture entries. High x-height and distinct glyph terminals ensure readability during low-brightness display operation.
- **Telemetry Monospace (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to technical real-time readings: GPS coordinates (`39°34'12"N 2°39'01"E`), water temperature, tidal height graphs, wind vectors in knots, sink rates (`s/m`), and catch mantle length counters. Monospaced tabular alignment prevents layout jitter when live telemetry updates.
- **All-Caps Micro Labels (`label-caps`):** Used on status tags, categorical groupings, and sensor headers to produce the feeling of military/marine deck hardware.

## Layout & Spacing

Field use requires high hit-target density paired with strict spatial modularity. Anglers often interact with wet hands or neoprene fingerless gloves; hence touch areas adhere to a minimum 48x48px bounding box regardless of visual padding.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Mobile:** 4-column fluid layout with `0.75rem` (12px) gutters and `1rem` (16px) outer edge margins.
  - **Tablet/Deck Display:** 8-column layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.5rem` (24px) margins.
  - **Desktop/Console:** 12-column fixed grid with a max width of 1280px for desktop analysis and session reviewing.
- **Rhythm & Stacking:** 
  - Standard spacing increments operate strictly on multiples of 4px/8px.
  - Vertical stacking between related telemetric readouts uses `space-xs` and `space-sm`.
  - Independent tactical telemetry cards use `space-md` separation to maintain clear cognitive borders in the dark.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than conventional drop shadows that mimic overhead sunlight, this design system creates depth through **tonal stratification**, **luminescent perimeter glow**, and **backlit optics**.

### Depth Layers
1. **The Depths (Base Canvas):** Solid `#070d18`. Serves as the backdrop for all interface elements.
2. **Structural Trench (Nested Inset):** Sunken fields or input areas use `#050912` with a 1px inner stroke of `#14223d` to signify receptive data slots.
3. **Floating Decks (Cards & Toolbars):** `#111c33` with a 1px perimeter border of `#1e335a`. Elevated panels rely on light-absorbing contrast rather than soft ambient drop shadows.
4. **Modal Overlays & Sticky Nav:** Glass-like translucency with `#0a1124` at 85% opacity, paired with an intensive `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle `#1e335a` bottom edge border.

### Luminescent Energy (Bioluminescent Glow)
Active status states and high-priority alerts use radiating luminescence:
- **Active Phosphor Ring:** `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(0, 229, 188, 0.28), inset 0 0 8px rgba(0, 229, 188, 0.12)`.
- **Lunar Solunar Peak:** `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(255, 158, 59, 0.32), inset 0 0 6px rgba(255, 158, 59, 0.15)`.
- Shadows are never pure neutral black; they are deep indigo-tinted occlusions (`rgba(4, 7, 14, 0.8)`).

## Shapes

The design system incorporates calibrated rounding (`roundedness: 2`, where base components feature `0.5rem` / 8px border radii). This prevents interface elements from feeling clinically sharp while avoiding the toy-like softness of full pill shapes.

### Corner Radii Hierarchy
- **Base Components (Inputs, Tactical Buttons, Data Cells):** `0.5rem` (8px). Provides crisp boundaries with humanized ergonomics.
- **Large Panels (Telemetry Cards, Catch Log Sheets):** `1rem` (16px) via `rounded-lg`.
- **System Sheets & Modals:** `1.5rem` (24px) via `rounded-xl` for comfortable physical dragging.
- **Functional Exceptions (Pills & Markers):** Status tags, moon-phase badges, tide counters, and sink-rate capsules employ continuous full-radius pill styling (`9999px`) to distinguish categorical tags from interactive action targets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Tactical Action ("Log Catch", "Start Session"):** Background gradient of `#00e5bc` to `#14f1d9`, text `#070d18` (`font-weight: 700`), with an outer aura glow of `rgba(0, 229, 188, 0.35)`. Subtle border in `#ffffff` at 20% opacity.
- **Secondary Instrumentation Action:** Background `#162442`, text `#f0f6fc`, border 1px solid `#1e335a`. Hover/Active: Border shifts to `#00e5bc` and text adopts a phosphor sheen.
- **Ghost/Utility Button:** Borderless, background transparent, text `#8b9eb7`. Hover: `#f0f6fc` with an icon illumination.

### Chips & Badges
- **Moon Phase & Solunar Pill:** Capsule shape (`9999px`), background `#162442`, border 1px solid `#374d6f`. Features an illuminated miniature lunar icon alongside monospace time countdowns in `#ff9e3b`.
- **Jig Spec Badge (Egi Size & Cloth Color):** High-density pill featuring a split swatch showing luminescent cloth type (e.g., Keimura UV, Glow Green, Marbled) beside the sink rating (`#8b9eb7` on `#111c33`).

### Telemetry Cards
- Containers use `#111c33` with crisp 1px borders in `#1e335a`.
- Header zone features the uppercase monospaced label (`label-caps`) paired with an icon colored according to metric state (Cyan for tides, Amber for solunar, Seafoam for barometric pressure).
- Content features large numeric readouts in `telemetry-lg` next to contextual trend arrows.
- Optional background includes an SVG bathymetric contour pattern watermark rendered at 4% opacity in `#00e5bc`.

### Tactical Catch Counter & Inputs
- **Mantle Length Stepper / Weight Stepper:** Large center-aligned numeric display in `JetBrains Mono` (`28px`), flanked by large 48x48px haptic minus/plus touch targets styled with `#162442` and glowing borders.
- **Form Fields:** Inset fill `#0a1124`, border 1px solid `#1e335a`, active focus ring radiating `#00e5bc` glow with `0 0 0 1px #00e5bc`. Text in `#f0f6fc` with placeholders in `#374d6f`.

### Lists & Catch Logs
- List items are arranged as distinct floating cards separated by `space-sm` rather than hairline dividers.
- Catch entries highlight the species silhouette (Calamar / Sepia / Totano), timestamp in monospaced seafoam, depth zone, and a luminous dot corresponding to the egi color used.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- **Toggle Switch:** Track filled with `#0a1124` when inactive; transitions to `#00e5bc` with a soft glow when active. Thumb is a solid, crisp circle in `#f0f6fc`.
- **Checkbox:** Square box with 4px corner radius, background `#0a1124`, border `#1e335a`. Checked state fills with `#00e5bc`, displaying an obsidian check mark icon.
